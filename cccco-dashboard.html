VR EEO Faculty Development & Peer Mentoring Program

A multi-college Virtual Reality faculty development and peer mentoring initiative across Bakersfield College, Cerro Coso College, and Porterville College — expanding equitable access to advanced professional development and strengthening the faculty pipeline from recruitment to retention.

Program Snapshot
CCCCO Aligned

Faculty across all disciplines complete a cohort-based, six-session VR training series (24 hours), pair with peer mentors, design and pilot VR-enhanced lessons, and contribute to a districtwide searchable lesson catalog. In Year 2, returning faculty advance into mentor roles, explore Unity-based VR content creation, or pursue larger innovations via Faculty Innovation Stipends.

The program is jointly led by Todd Coston (Associate Vice Chancellor of Planning & Educational Technology) and Rachel Tatro-Duarte (faculty co-chair, VR research). It aligns with Kern CCD's 2025–2030 Strategic Plan and strengthens the current EEO Plan by adding institutionalized post-hire professional development across all three colleges.

Project Goals

Four Goals Aligned to CCCCO EEO Priorities

Each goal addresses an identified gap in employment practices and post-hire faculty advancement, per the 2022 EEO/Diversity Best Practices Handbook.

  1. 1
    Reduce instructional technology access gaps across Kern CCD's three colleges
    Ensure faculty at smaller colleges (Cerro Coso, Porterville) have equal opportunity to experiment with and adopt immersive learning tools, eliminating disparities that limit career advancement based on campus assignment rather than merit.
  2. 2
    Promote inclusive, equity-focused teaching through VR-based pedagogy
    Equip faculty with strategies that foster empathy, cultural understanding, and engagement among diverse student populations, while simultaneously developing leadership skills in diversity, equity, and inclusion.
  3. 3
    Increase faculty confidence and cross-disciplinary use of VR teaching methods
    Expand instructional innovation beyond STEM fields so faculty build documented technology expertise that strengthens competitiveness for tenure, promotion, and leadership positions.
  4. 4
    Close a critical gap in Kern CCD's 2024–2027 EEO Plan
    Institutionalize structured post-hire professional development and retention strategies across all three colleges — moving beyond pre-hire activities to sustained career advancement pipelines.

A 6-month prep period (FY25–26) followed by two full academic years of cohort training, mentoring, classroom implementation, and showcases.

Year 1 · 2026–2027 Activities
  • A
    VR Training Curriculum
    Six 4-hour PD sessions covering VR hardware/software, pedagogy, curriculum design, and inclusive VR lesson planning.
  • B
    Peer Mentoring Pairs
    Each participating faculty member is paired with a trained peer mentor for course-specific guidance.
  • C
    Classroom Implementation
    Spring 2027: faculty pilot VR-enhanced lessons via several instructional models (full-class VR, individual exploration, pre/post integration).
  • D
    End-of-Year Showcase
    Internal conference-style event; standardized lesson templates capture pedagogical insights for future faculty use.
Year 2 · 2027–2028 Activities
  • A
    Mentor Program Expansion
    New mentees join alongside returning participants who advance into mentor or content-creation tracks.
  • B
    Advanced VR Design (Unity)
    Returning faculty design and test their own interactive VR experiences in Fall 2027 and implement in Spring 2028.
  • C
    Faculty Innovation Stipends
    Pool of stipend hours for larger-scale projects beyond core curriculum requirements ($30/hr).
  • D
    Final Showcase & Catalog
    Documented VR lessons organized into a searchable district-wide collection, extending impact beyond the grant.

Measurable Outcomes

Assessment Plan & Success Metrics

Five outcome areas with quantitative targets, evaluated each semester. Data disaggregated by college, discipline, and faculty demographic group.

Access & Equity
30+
Faculty trained across all 3 colleges, with participation proportional to FTES (±5%) and ≥35% non-STEM representation.
PD Effectiveness
≥75%
Participants report increased confidence using VR (pre/post survey); ≥60% implement a VR-enhanced lesson within the grant year.
Knowledge Sharing
35+
Searchable VR lessons documented; ≥30% adopted across campuses (developed at one college, implemented at another).
Leadership Pipeline
≥40%
Year-1 participants advance to mentor roles in Year 2; ≥25% of alumni document new committee/leadership service in portfolios.
Student Engagement
≥70%
Students in VR-enhanced sections report increased engagement, with no demographic engagement gap >10 pts.
Data Collection & Use

Mentor/mentee logs, standardized lesson documentation, participant pre/post surveys, and brief student pulse surveys feed into the grant leadership team's semester-end disaggregation by college, discipline, and faculty demographic group. Findings are summarized annually and inform Kern CCD's broader EEO and professional development strategies.
