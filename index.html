Goals & Objectives

Four program goals anchor the VR EEO Faculty Development initiative. Each goal pairs with measurable objectives tied to deliverables in the workplan.

  • 1
    Expand access to VR-driven pedagogy for KCCD faculty
    Deploy 60 Meta Quest 3 VR headsets across the three colleges and run a 6-session VR pedagogy training series. Builds faculty fluency with immersive tools and reduces friction to classroom adoption.
  • 2
    Build a sustainable faculty peer mentoring network
    Pair experienced faculty with newer hires across the district. Quarterly peer mentor cohorts, with a focus on EEO-aligned recruitment and retention practices.
  • 3
    Produce a shared catalog of district-wide VR lessons
    Faculty contribute VR-based lessons to a shared catalog tagged by discipline and EEO competency. Catalog is published district-wide by end of Year 1.
  • 4
    Disseminate findings and build long-term capacity
    Year 2 emphasizes scaling: faculty-led Unity VR content creation training, public-facing case studies, and a partner playbook for adoption beyond KCCD.
